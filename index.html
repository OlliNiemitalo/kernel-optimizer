Circular blur kernel optimizer

Disc kernels as sums of phased Gaussians (cos(c r²)·rw + sin(c r²)·iw)·e−b r² and, optionally, merged pairs (repeated rates) with an extra r² term, each separable into horizontal and vertical complex 1-D passes. Global search by differential evolution and gradient fine-tuning on jax-js-nonconsuming; the blur demo is plain WebGPU shaders. After Olli Niemitalo: Circularly symmetric convolution and lens blur.

About multiplicities

Per component: 1 = single, 2 = merged pair, i.e. two coinciding rates in closed form, (B + A r²)·eλr², costing two complex channels per pass. Example: 2, 2, 1. More in the README.

Target and error
About negative gain and search ranges

Errors where the kernel is negative count "negative gain" times. Above 1, the optimizers trade ripple for fewer negative values, which look like cheap sharpening (as in the article's non-negative sets).

Gradient fine-tuning
How it works

Minimizes a log-sum-exp smooth maximum of |error| on the dense grid with Adam in parameter-relative units (learning rate defaults to 0.001/3N−2 and halves every third of the run; sharpness, relative to the current ripple, rises 10×). Keeps the best kernel by true maximum error.

2-D kernel normalized to its peak: positive values in gray levels, negative values in red, on the same brightness scale.
