Circular blur kernel optimizer

Disc kernels as sums of phased Gaussians (cos(c r²)·rw + sin(c r²)·iw)·e−b r² and, optionally, merged pairs (repeated rates) with an extra r² term, each separable into horizontal and vertical complex 1-D passes. Global search by differential evolution and gradient fine-tuning on jax-js-nonconsuming; the blur demo is plain WebGPU shaders. After Olli Niemitalo: Circularly symmetric convolution and lens blur. Gradient fine-tuning

Minimizes a log-sum-exp smooth maximum of |error| on the dense grid with Adam in parameter-relative units (learning rate defaults to 0.001/3N−2 and halves every third of the run; sharpness, relative to the current ripple, rises 10×). Keeps the best kernel by true maximum error.
